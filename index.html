Privacy Policy

What aivod does

aivod is a personal video-publishing tool. Its owner uses it to create short educational videos about personal finance (budgeting, saving, debt, credit and investing basics) and publish them to the owner's own YouTube channel.

For each video, aivod writes a short script, produces a voiceover and captions, combines them with licensed stock footage, and uploads the finished video to YouTube with a title, description and tags.

How aivod uses Google user data

aivod signs in with Google only to upload videos to the signed-in user's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, and to read that channel's name so the user can confirm where uploads go. It does not access any other Google data. The sign-in token stays on the owner's own computer and is never shared. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Who can use it

aivod is not a public service. It is run only by its owner, on the owner's computer, for the owner's channel. No login is needed to view this page.

Videos published with aivod are for educational purposes only and are not financial advice.
